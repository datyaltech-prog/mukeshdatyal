Building DatyalTech

DatyalTech is my independent software brand where I build and publish practical Android applications and digital tools.

The idea is simple: take an everyday problem, remove unnecessary complexity, and turn it into a useful product. Rather than adding bloated features or third-party ad networks that track users, each utility focuses on doing its primary task reliably, quickly, and often completely offline.

Philosophy

How I Build

A straightforward, personal approach to making software that people can count on.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Find the actual problem

Strip away noise to identify what calculation, action, or workflow is currently tedious or confusing for users.

02 — DESIGN

Simple user experience

Design clean inputs, unambiguous results, and immediate responses without unnecessary animation or friction.

03 — BUILD

Develop, test & refine

Write robust Kotlin/Dart, verify offline handling, test edge cases, and ensure minimal battery and memory overhead.

04 — SHIP

Publish & improve

Ship to Google Play, monitor real crash metrics and user reviews, and refine the tool based on actual utility.
